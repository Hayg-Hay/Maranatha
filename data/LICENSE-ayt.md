Conversion: Scripture text comes from the publisher JSON, unchanged except
removing `<t />` heading-placement markup. Footnotes, cross-references and
headings are retained separately from the same commit's SFM files. Three
source reference pointers remain exact strings; no absent text is inferred.
The publisher's Psalm superscriptions and alternate labels within numbered
text remain there verbatim. Independent checks compare every numbered text
record with the original CSV and cross-check SFM references and base wording.
Formatting differences among the publisher's exports are recorded rather
than repaired. No translation or editorial corrections were made.
