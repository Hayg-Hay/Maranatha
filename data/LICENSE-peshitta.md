# Syriac Peshitta: CrossWire BFBS-labelled digital NT

Original source: CrossWire Peshitta 2.0 (2020-02-08), language syr, RTL.
The distributor declares **Public Domain** and identifies the BFBS 1905 text,
courtesy John Richards. The named Roger Pearse upstream page also declares
Public Domain and credits Richards's upload. These are source declarations,
not a new worldwide copyright determination or proof of a complete print match.

- https://www.crosswire.org/sword/modules/ModInfo.jsp?modName=Peshitta
- https://www.tertullian.org/rpearse/thesyriaclibrary/new_testament.htm

Unchanged archive/markup and an upstream capture are pinned in
`build/sources/peshitta`. The module contains 27 NT books and no OT text.
Five books are later-supplied Syriac books, outside the traditional 22-book
Peshitta corpus. Their precise printed recensions are not independently
certified; per-book provenance notes disclose this. The complete BFBS NT
tradition incorporates material later than its 1905 Gospel publication, so
the module's date is recorded as its declaration, not an invented exact
27-book print impression.

Changes: technical conversion only. Structural tags become metadata, and the
explicit source marker 50 separates Mark 9:50 from indexed slot 49. All Syriac
words, punctuation and code points remain unchanged. No MarYa substitution,
English translation or new word-annotation layer was added.

The local Noto Sans Syriac Western font is independently licensed under SIL
OFL 1.1; see `fonts/NotoSansSyriacWestern-OFL.txt`. Code2000 is not bundled.
