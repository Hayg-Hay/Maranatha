# Bohairica 1.2 — Coptic Bohairic New Testament

Edition: **Bohairica: The Bohairic New Testament in Standardized Form**,
1.2 (2006.December.14), prepared by **J. Warren Wells**, based on **Horner**.
Distributor: **eBible.org**, identifier `copbhc` / COPBHC.

unchanged in `build/sources/bohairica/copyright.htm`, `usfm/copr.htm` and

Retrieved 2026-10-18. The notice identifies standardized paragraphing and
punctuation and expanded sacred-name abbreviations. This is Wells' edited
Bohairica, not an unaltered Horner transcription or a certified official church
edition. The package is NT-only. Source letters, case, internal spacing,
punctuation and numbered units are preserved; no missing wording is supplied.

The unchanged source archives, all extracted files and metadata are SHA-256
pinned by `build/sources/bohairica/source-files.json`. The importer pins that
manifest as well. Six source records contain only a bracketed empty marker
(`[ ]` at Mark 9:44, Mark 9:46, Luke 17:36, Acts 8:37, Acts 28:29 and
Revelation 12:18); they are kept exactly and tagged as source placeholders, not
as supplied Scripture. Five records containing the source's own `]-` character
sequence (Mark 6:48, John 13:3, 2 Timothy 2:25, Revelation 4:9 and Revelation
13:2) are likewise preserved verbatim without correction. Acts 24:7 remains the
only numeric gap. See `docs/BOHAIRICA.md` for the independent verification
results and remaining limits.
