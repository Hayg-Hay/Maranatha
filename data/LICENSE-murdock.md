# James Murdock's English Syriac NT

Source: CrossWire Murdock 1.2 (2002-01-01), whose configuration declares
**Public Domain**, publication 1852, and source peshito.com. Original archive,
GBF notes, appendix and conversion errata are retained under
`build/sources/murdock`. This records distributor provenance and declaration.

- https://www.crosswire.org/sword/modules/ModInfo.jsp?modName=Murdock
- Authorial-preface reproduction: https://aramaicnewtestament.org/peshitta/murdock/

The reproduced 1852 preface identifies the BFBS Syriac editions of 1816 and
1826, with Leusden/Schaaf 1717 and Gutbir consultation. This English work is
not a translation made from the later BFBS text used by our Syriac module.
It has a separate source identity and its own native reference placement.

Changes: technical conversion only. Nineteen GBF footnotes remain separate;
italic markup is flattened without removing its words. The module's errata
records appended native Romans 7:26, 3 John 1:15 and Revelation 12:18. Their
explicit converter-added labels and outer framing are retained as provenance
and used to restore native units. All English wording and authorial punctuation
content stay intact. Lord/THE LORD is not replaced with MarYa.

Ten empty indexed slots remain unfilled. Their classification as actual
omission or index/boundary residue awaits source verification; no text from
another edition has been supplied.
