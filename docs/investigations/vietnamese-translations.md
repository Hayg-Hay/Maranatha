## Recommended modern candidate

**Biblica Open Vietnamese Contemporary Bible 2015 (OVCB)**, Vietnamese title
**Biblica® Thiên Ban Kinh Thánh Hiện Đại™**. Biblica's own current reader
explicitly lists the open edition and the year 2015. eBible's `vieovcb` entry
provides the full copyright/title/trademark notice and CC BY-SA 4.0 terms,
plus structured USFM and USFX archives. Copyright years are 1982, 1987, 1994,
2005, 2015. The distributor's page update date is not a new translation year.

This is a modern-reading candidate, consistent with the user's recent choices
of Thai TCV and Filipino ASD. Readability preference is a recommendation, not
a native-speaker assessment or certification of translation accuracy. Do not
invent a 2025 Vietnamese edition or treat every platform's similarly titled
Kinh Thánh Hiện Đại text as the same pinned artifact.

Sources:

- https://www.biblica.com/online-bible/
- https://ebible.org/bible/details.php?id=vieovcb
- https://www.biblica.com/bible/ovcb/s%C3%A1ng-th%E1%BA%BF-k%C3%BD/2/
- USFM: https://ebible.org/Scriptures/vieovcb_usfm.zip
- USFX: https://ebible.org/Scriptures/vieovcb_usfx.zip

## Historical companion

eBible's `vie1934` artifact is labelled Vietnamese Bible 1923 / Kinh Thánh,
with an explanatory notice: NT first published 1923, OT first published 1925.
It declares Public Domain and supplies USFM/USFX. Its artifact id contains
1934; record that identity without silently renaming its publication history.
Its quoted samples use older wording than OVCB. Keep later revisions distinct.

Source: https://ebible.org/details.php?id=vie1934

## Other modern editions
