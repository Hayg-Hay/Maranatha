## Edition identity result

This is an identified original **1853 print**, but it is **not a verbatim
wording match for the current ArmWestern module**. The differences in the
inspected Gospel passages include lexical choices, forms and word order, not
just capitalization, normalization or quote characters.

The module's 40 wording anchors match the separate 2007 electronic witness
(37 strict diagnostic matches, 40 after quote folding). That establishes a
digital family, not identity with this printed edition. The source may be a
later revision/modernization carrying an 1853-family label. That hypothesis
requires bibliographic evidence and must not be turned into an invented date
or definitive revision attribution.

John 6 re-merging is supported against the repeated 6:51 labels in the digital
witness. It does not reproduce this print's native 51/52 and 72-unit extent.
Those two findings are compatible once the witnesses are kept separate.

## Decision from this pilot

1. Preserve the historical scan as an identified full Western print base.
2. Keep the existing digital NT and its committed repairs as a distinct,
   under-audit digital witness. Its "1853" label is distributor metadata,
   not yet verified identity with this actual 1853 print.
3. Do **not** fill its Corinthians gaps from this different wording, reverse
   its repairs to imitate print labels, or merge print OT with the digital NT
   under one supposedly homogeneous edition.
4. Seek the digital family's exact revision/source from its distributor or
   original publisher. The existing CrossWire/Unbound lineage does not name it.
5. If a genuine 1853 text edition is selected, it requires its own native units,
   labels and reviewed transcription. A raw OCR pass cannot deliver a verified
   66-book Bible. Routine page processing can be handed to DeepSeek, but text
   acceptance requires reliable Armenian print comparison.
