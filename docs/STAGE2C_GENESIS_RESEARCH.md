- **Existing source 6:1** contains target 5:32 and 6:1. Reuse that row intact;
  never duplicate it in the new ledger or discard its cross-chapter targets.
- **Source 15:18** contains the covenant/land boundaries followed by the peoples
  listed at targets 15:19–21. It includes literal `§20` inside its text, not a
  separate native verse label. Investigate an explicit one-source/many-target
  proposal using exact per-target evidence. Show the whole source once, with
  continuation notes. Do not invent source labels 19–21 or split source text.
- **Source 11:12–13** names Cainan; source 11:13 contains Arphaxad's later years
  AND Cainan's begetting/later years. WEB/KJV/OSHB 11:12–13 describe Arphaxad
  begetting Shelah and his later years. Ages also differ. Same genealogy context
  does not prove identical father/son statements. Record the extra generation
  and differences; leave uncertain proposals unresolved rather than force them.
- **Source 7:11** has twenty-seventh-day wording versus seventeenth in inspected
  comparison corpora; its opening/month and ending also appear incomplete.
  Inspect the adjacent source units before deciding coverage or a notice.
- **Source 8:22** ends literally `οὐ κατα- IX`; source 9:1 starts literally
  `IXκαὶ εἶπεν αὐτοῖς` and lacks the inspected English/Hebrew opening blessing
  clause. Existing mixed-script flags are attached. Do not complete the broken
  word or restore missing wording from memory. Do not assume the missing text
  is present in the next container without inspecting it.
- **Source 10:1** has `Σῆμ’, Χάμ, ’σαν` where the comparison has the three sons
  and the begetting statement. Preserve the actual transcription; investigate
  and disclose incomplete readings instead of silently presenting full equivalence.
- Inspect genealogy ages across chapter 11, all chapter transitions, and other
  material wording differences using this witness, not a remembered LXX edition.

These are observations of THIS shipped transcription. They do not establish
