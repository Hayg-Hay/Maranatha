## Collective group 6–7 (the one non-singleton)

Greek source 6 ends with **καὶ ἐγένετο οὕτως**; WEB, KJV and OSHB place the
fulfillment formula **"and it was so"** at target 7. Source 7 then narrates making
the expanse and separating the waters. I verified the clause boundary in all four
strings. The two source verses and the two target verses form one passage, so the
collective group is correct; it must never be rendered as exact 6=6 / 7=7
boundaries. This is the only grouping in the pilot and it is preserved.
